WHAT THIS PROTOTYPE IS
Live reference implementation

The scoring, grading, auto-comment, draft-recovery and offline-queue logic here is real and runs in the browser — it is the behaviour the Next.js build ports directly.

Offline-first by design
Switch to Offline in the top bar and mark a whole assessment — nothing degrades. Submit, and the finished report waits in Pending until you switch back to Online.
Sequential marking
Try tapping Next with a criterion unmarked — the section will not release you until every criterion has a score.
Try draft recovery
Score a few criteria, tap Trainees to leave mid-assessment, then reopen that trainee — Tathmini offers to resume.
Grading key in force
A 80–100% · B 65–79% · C 50–64% · D 40–49% · F 0–39%. Class of Award and GPA on A/B/C only. Competent at ≥ 50%.
Test a reassignment
Open Moves — a request from Route 03 is waiting. Accept it and Rehema joins your route immediately. Or start your own from any trainee's profile.
See the PDF
The report opens inside the app — from Preview the PDF report before submitting, from View attachment after, or from a trainee's record on the Coordinator side.
