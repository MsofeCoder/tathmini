It is completed at the industry during the supervision visit, from direct observation of the trainee at work, inspection of the work produced, questioning at the workstation, examination of the industrial records kept, and consultation with the Industrial Supervisor. Require the trainee to carry out at least one occupation-specific industrial operation in your presence. An assessment made without observing work being performed is not valid. Tick (✓) one box per item, corresponding to the trainee’s performance of that industrial practice. Every item carries 5 marks. Add the item marks to obtain each section total, then the GRAND TOTAL.
